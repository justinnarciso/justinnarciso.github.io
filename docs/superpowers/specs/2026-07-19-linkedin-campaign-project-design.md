# Project 04: LinkedIn Content Campaign — Design

Date: 2026-07-19 · Approved by user

## Goal
Add `linkedin_content_campaign.pdf` (already in repo) as the fourth project on
`projects.html`, with a graphics-forward layout since this project's work
product is visual.

## Design
- Fourth full-width tile after PlayStation, before the footer. Dark tile
  (`tile-dark`) restoring the light/dark alternation. Label
  `Project 04 · July 2026`, tape accent, whole tile links to the PDF.
- Left column: `no. 04` hero number, title "NYC Energy Firm · LinkedIn
  Content Campaign", two blurbs (campaign summary; AI-assisted process +
  per-post visual concepts).
- Right column: the 3 campaign graphics as polaroid-style snapshots with
  cream borders, alternating slight rotations, soft shadows — replacing the
  stats grid used by other tiles. Collapses to a row/stack on mobile.
- Chips: Content Strategy · LinkedIn · Copywriting · Canva · AI-Assisted,
  plus handwritten "view full campaign →".
- Images extracted from the PDF, resized/compressed for web, stored in
  `assets/` (campaign_post1.jpg, campaign_post2.jpg, campaign_post3.jpg).
- No changes to projects 01–03.

## Known limitation
Source graphics carry a faint Canva watermark; barely visible at thumbnail
size. Swap in clean exports later if available.
